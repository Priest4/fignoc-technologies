# Keyword research — Fignoc Technologies

Target: people in Zimbabwe who need a **website, software or an app**, and who
are close enough to buying that they will send a message.

Researched August 2026 against live SERPs. Re-check quarterly — this market's
pricing pages change often.

---

## What this document is, and what it is not

**It is** a keyword universe grouped by search intent, mapped to the pages you
already have, with the gaps named. Every competitor and price quoted below came
from a live search result, and every URL was checked.

**It is not** a volume report. Nobody can give you honest monthly search volumes
for `.zw` queries without Google Keyword Planner or a paid tool, and inventing
them would be worse than leaving them out. Two things fix that, in order:

1. **Verify Search Console.** The moment `fignoc.co.zw` verifies, the
   Performance report gives you *real* queries that already bring impressions —
   the single most reliable keyword source you can have, because it is your own
   traffic rather than an estimate.
2. **Open Keyword Planner** (free with any Google Ads account, even unfunded)
   and paste the seed terms in section 3. Set location Zimbabwe, language
   English. It returns ranges, not exact figures, which is honest enough.

You already have a proof of this working: Recruitment263's Search Console shows
12,500 impressions a month at average position 6. That property is your best
evidence of how Zimbabweans actually phrase searches.

## 1. The market you are ranking against

Real prices from live competitor pages, August 2026:

| Competitor | Entry price | Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| Modern Solutions | from **$40** | cheapest found; hosting from $4/mo |
| Syrup (Harare) | from **$120** | $240 for "dynamic" |
| iNetAxis | from **$250** | "affordable, professional" |
| TechTribe | from **$350**, standard **$550**, premium **$800+** | strongest pricing-page SEO of the group |
| Published cost guides | $150–$350 basic, $350–$900+ professional | |

**What this means for positioning.** Your FAQ says *"Around Harare you'll find
templates from $50 and agency builds from $350 to $900."* That holds up — but
$80 is **not** the cheapest in the market, and a page that leans on being cheap
will lose to the $40 option. The defensible ground is the one the landing page
already takes: measurement, ownership and speed at a price near the bottom of
the range. Compete on "what happens after launch", never on price alone.

**The competitor to study is TechTribe.** They rank for the money terms because
they have a dedicated `/pricing` page and a `/blog/website-cost-zimbabwe-2026`
article. That is the exact pattern to copy — and `/website-design` is already
most of the way there.

**Software and apps are a different, weaker field.** The custom-software SERP is
mostly directories (TechBehemoths, Clouditate, Twine) rather than strong local
pages, and app-cost content is thin. Directories ranking above real companies
means the on-page competition is beatable.

App pricing found: **$800–$1,200** typical, quotes spanning $500 to $50,000+.

## 5. AEO — the part nobody local is doing

Your differentiator is real and the competition is close to zero. AI answers are
won by pages that state a fact plainly, in the words of the question, with a
number attached.

Questions to answer verbatim as headings, each followed by a direct sentence:

- How much does a website cost in Zimbabwe?
- Who builds websites in Harare?
- What is the best way to accept EcoCash payments online?
- How long does it take to build a website?
- Do I need a .co.zw domain?

The pattern that gets cited: **question as an H2, answer in the first sentence
under it, a specific number in that sentence.** Your `/website-design` FAQ
already does this. Extend it to the service pages.

## 6. Index problems found while researching

Checked live, August 2026.

| Finding | Detail | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **`/shop/` is indexed but 404s** | Google still lists `fignoc.co.zw/shop/`. It returns **404**. | 301 it to `/products`, or leave it to drop out. A 301 recovers any link equity. |
| **`fignocsearch.co.zw` is indexed but dead** | Five results under a second brand ("Fignoc Search — Gift Simau"). The domain **404s**. | Decide: revive, 301 to `fignoc.co.zw`, or let it die. Right now it splits brand searches and shows dead pages for "Fignoc". |
| **Google has the pre-fix titles** | The homepage still shows the 86-character title in results. | Expected — four builds are undeployed. |
| **`/website-design` in the sitemap at 0.9** | Competes with `/services/web-development`. | Decide: organic asset or paid-only. If paid-only, `noindex` and drop from the sitemap. |

## 7. Priority order

1. **Verify Search Console.** Everything else is guesswork until real query data
   arrives. The TXT record is the blocker.
2. **Deploy the four pending builds** — the duplicate `/work` vs `/products`
   titles are live cannibalisation right now.
3. **Create the app development service page.** You sell it and have no page.
4. **Build `/pricing`.** One page, three lines of business.
5. **Write the website-cost article.** Highest-value Tier 2 term.
6. **Set up Google Business Profile** — "near me" and map-pack queries are Tier 1
   and cannot be won without it.
7. **Thicken `/services/ecommerce`** around Paynow and EcoCash.
8. **Sector pages**, three to start.
9. **Resolve `fignocsearch.co.zw`** and 301 `/shop/`.

Items 1, 2, 6 and 9 are configuration. Items 3–5, 7 and 8 are content, and are
where the compounding returns are.
